LE PUB

THE CHASE

Une chasse sans pitié… serve the regulars, dodge the hunter.

  1. Spot a ticket
  2. Grab it at its counter
  3. Serve it. Dodge him.

HOUSE RULES

THE JOB

A ticket over someone's head is an order. The bar on top is their patience: green, amber, then red.

+18+5 CLUTCH-15

Fresher means bigger tips: 10 to 20. A save in the red pays a clutch bonus. Let a ticket run out and it costs you 15.

SHIFT 1TIPS 60/100

Every shift has a tips target. Make it before closing time.

THE BAR

TAPSSHELFKITCHEN

Beer and water at the taps. Wine and cocktails at the shelf. Food at the kitchen. A ticket's coloured corner tells you which.

TAPS3

The number on a counter is how many orders are waiting there. The ringed counter has the oldest one: go there next.

EGRAB

Stand at the counter and press the button to grab. Walk it over and press again to serve. The prompt by your head says which.

The tray holds two. A full one slows you down, but landing both without getting caught pays a double bonus.

THE HUNTER

He walks in a little after opening and prowls. He only sees what's in front of him, or hears you right behind him.

!

A ! means he's seen you and he's coming. A red glow at the screen edge means he's close; an arrow shows where.

Each catch costs one of your three pints. Keep clear for a few seconds and they refill. Lose all three and it's over.

He orders pints too (red ticket). Serve him and he sits down to drink it. Good tip, and a breather for you.

PERKS

About two drink deliveries in five earn you a Jameson: 10 seconds where he can't touch you. He runs, and bumping him bounces him off.

Every 5 deliveries you pocket a pack of smokes (up to 3). Drop one in his path and he steps outside for a cigarette.

THE BOOTH

Sam, Nazim and Gerald own the corner booth all night. Amber tickets are theirs. Gerald is the least patient man in the building.

Every drink moves Nazim along, sober to gone. Drunk Nazim tips double but knocks pints over, and spills slow everyone down. Once he's gone, a water sobers him up.

+25

Now and then they call a round: serve all three within 20 seconds for a bonus.

CHAOS

TO THE WC 42S

Three Jamesons fill the bladder. You then have 60 seconds to reach the WC (bottom right). Miss it: -20 tips and a puddle the busboy has to mop.

Alex drops by to do the splits. An amber outline warns you first, then he blocks that spot for a few seconds.

Jay the waiter wipes down the bar, and the pub has a ghost. Both are harmless. Probably.

SHIFTS

TOTAL 2400:12

The first five shifts last 3 minutes. The last 15 seconds are last call: no new customers, and patience drains faster.

2

Every shift is busier and the hunter sharper. From shift 6 there's no clock: just the target. How far can you go?
